## 1. Daniel Okafor, the stuck junior

- Pains: "I have been junior for two years and nobody tells me what mid-level means." "My reviews take days and say little." "I never design anything, I just follow the ticket."

- Discovery: Reddit, Discord, peers. Would try it free on a peer's word. Would pay if a hiring manager valued the record.
- Fit: urgency 4, willingness to pay 3, reachability 3. Primary.
